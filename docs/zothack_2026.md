**Mentor** · ZotHack 2026 (SignLingo, Best Overall Winner)
Irvine, CA (In person at UCI) · Oct 2026 – Oct 2026

Mentored the three-student team behind SignLingo, the Best Overall winner at ZotHack 2026. SignLingo is a Mac app that helps you learn ASL fingerspelling with your webcam: Learn Letters mode shows each sign and tells you when you've got it right, and Spell Words mode has you fingerspell whole words.

**Highlights**

- Guided Karen Huang, Etinosa Ogbeide, and Jeffrey Song to a Best Overall win at ZotHack 2026 with an app that recognizes all 26 letters live.
- Wrote a step-by-step build guide taking beginners from a webcam feed to live letter checks, testing each step.
- Planned the 12-hour build across camera, data, and UI/pitch roles, with checkpoints and backup plans for each.
- Reviewed the team's code throughout the hackathon to keep the codebase clean and well organized.
- Coached the team for judging, from the live demo and Devpost write-up to explaining their own code.
- Published the v1.0.0 Mac app release and wrote its install guide.
